# APS Drone Researched Outreach Templates v5.6

These are frameworks, not bulk-mail copy. Every message requires a verified recipient, one genuine personalization trigger, the correct service hypothesis, and a CRM entry. The canonical phone and mailing address are included below; still check every recipient against Opt Out / Do Not Contact before sending.

## Pre-send gate

1. Confirm the company, website, recipient name/role, and email are current.
2. Replace every `{{variable}}`; never send a placeholder.
3. Use one factual trigger from the prospect's own site or a current project/listing.
4. Link only the most relevant APS proof page; do not attach large media.
5. Set the CRM next-action date before sending.
6. Include the compliance footer below.

## Priority gaps to close

1. Reviews: request fair Google reviews from completed customers; never gate, buy or script positive reviews.
2. Recurring work: add a construction-progress cadence example and a simple monthly-scope explanation after a real project supports it.
3. Commercial real estate: publish a client-approved CRE case study with objective, shot list, turnaround and deliverables.
4. FPV proof: add one polished local business-tour case study and a 20–40 second preview with a descriptive transcript.
5. Local authority: complete accurate, consistent profiles on legitimate DFW and industry directories; avoid mass directory spam.
6. Conversion evidence: measure quote submits, phone, text, WhatsApp and email clicks in GA4 before any paid campaign.

APS Drone Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 16, 2026

Information submitted with a quote request

When you request a quote, APS Drone receives the information you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, company, project location, requested service, preferred date, budget range, referral source and project message.

Attribution and website information

The site may record the landing page, referring page and campaign parameters such as UTM values or advertising click identifiers. First-touch attribution may be stored in your browser's local storage so APS Drone can understand which marketing source led to a request.

How the information is used

APS Drone uses this information to respond to requests, assess flight feasibility, prepare a scope or quote, schedule work, improve marketing performance and maintain business records. APS Drone does not sell quote-request information.

Service providers

Website form submissions are processed by Formspree. If analytics is enabled, aggregated website activity may also be processed through Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics. Those providers handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and choices

Business inquiry records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for the request, customer relationship, accounting, legal or operational purposes. You may ask APS Drone to correct or delete contact information when no longer required to retain it.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, call or text (832) 938-9570, or use the website quote form and select “Other.”

Commercial drone service questions

What commercial properties can APS Drone photograph?

Industrial buildings, warehouses, offices, retail, multifamily, hospitality, venues, campuses, land and development sites across DFW.

How much does commercial aerial photography cost?

Commercial work is quoted by scope because property size, access, airspace, usage, deliverables and timing vary.

Can you provide a certificate of insurance?

Yes. APS Drone is insured and can provide a certificate of insurance when appropriate for project or vendor review.

Does roof photography count as an inspection?

No. APS Drone provides visual capture and organized documentation. Interpretation should be completed by the client’s qualified inspector, engineer or other professional.

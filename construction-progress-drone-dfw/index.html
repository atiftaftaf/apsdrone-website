Construction progress drone questions

How often can progress flights be scheduled?

Weekly, biweekly and monthly plans are available. Actual flights remain subject to airspace, weather, access and site safety.

Can you use the same viewpoints every visit?

Yes, agreed views are repeated when operationally feasible. Active equipment, cranes, temporary restrictions or safety conditions may require adjustments.

Is this a survey or engineering inspection?

No. APS Drone provides visual documentation and mapping capture support, not a licensed land survey, engineering opinion or code inspection.

How is construction progress pricing determined?

Pricing depends on the site, flight frequency, travel, access, airspace, number of viewpoints, photo or video deliverables and project duration.

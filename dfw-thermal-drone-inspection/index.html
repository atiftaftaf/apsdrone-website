Thermal drone imaging questions

What deliverables are available?

Scope can include matched JPEG visual and thermal images plus radiometric files when the camera, workflow and client review software support them.

Can a thermal image alone identify a leak or defect?

No. Heat patterns can have multiple causes and must be evaluated with site conditions and other evidence by a qualified professional.

Where does APS Drone provide thermal capture?

Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Irving, Plano, Frisco and surrounding DFW communities, subject to airspace and site feasibility.

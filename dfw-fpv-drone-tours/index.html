How an FPV project works

1. Review the space

Share the location, audience, platform and must-show areas.

2. Plan the route

APS Drone reviews access, obstacles, people and safe control points.

3. Rehearse and capture

The route is walked, timed and captured under controlled conditions.

4. Edit and deliver

Receive the agreed vertical or horizontal edit and revision.

FPV drone tour questions

Is every business suitable for an indoor FPV flight?

No. Space, occupants, route clearance, property permission, safety controls and operational feasibility must be reviewed first.

Can employees or customers appear in the video?

Only when participation, permissions, route control and the production plan are agreed in advance.

Can FPV be combined with exterior aerial video?

Yes. When feasible, an exterior establishing sequence can be combined with the interior tour and ground-camera content.
